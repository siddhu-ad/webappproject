Our story

The circle began as a handful of BIT students sharing notes before exams. Sessions soon turned into hands-on practice, and the group grew into a weekly meetup open to students from any college in the valley.

We keep things informal. Every session has one topic, one exercise and time to ask questions without feeling rushed.
